* Ordinary slides

Everything between a section heading and the first slide becomes that
section slide's subtitle.

** A bulleted slide

A level-two heading is a content slide; Mosaic builds it from the
heading itself, so nothing else is needed.

- Ordinary Org markup, *bold* and /italic/
- Inline code =like this= and math $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$
- Links to [[https://typst.app][Typst]]

*** A deeper heading

A heading below the slide level stays an ordinary heading inside the
slide body rather than starting a new slide.

* Slide layouts

Headline properties carry Mosaic's layout fields.

** Comparison

needed: the split alone is enough.

** Quotations

A plain quote block stays a native Typst quote, styled by the theme.

#+begin_quote
The estimate is positive.
#+end_quote

An =#+ATTR_MOSAIC:= line turns it into Mosaic's quote component. Values
there are Typst, so =_..._= italicises a title.

#+ATTR_MOSAIC: :attribution Ada Lovelace :source _Notes on the Analytical Engine_
#+begin_quote
The Analytical Engine weaves algebraic patterns.
#+end_quote
